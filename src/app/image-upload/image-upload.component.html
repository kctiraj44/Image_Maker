🇺🇸 US Passport Photo Requirements

Official requirements from the U.S. Department of State for a 2×2 inch (51×51 mm) digital photo. Unacceptable photos are the #1 reason applications are put on hold.

📐 Size 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm)
👤 Head Height 50%–69% of total image height
🖼️ Digital image Square · 600–1200 px · JPEG
📅 Recency Taken within last 6 months

Background

  • ✅ Plain white or off-white background
  • ✅ No shadows, textures, lines, or objects
  • ❌ No colored or patterned backgrounds

Size & Position

  • ✅ Head 50%–69% of total image height
  • ✅ Full-face view directly facing the camera
  • ✅ Both eyes open and fully visible
  • ❌ No tilted or turned head

Shadows & Lighting

  • ✅ Even lighting across the face
  • ✅ No shadows on face or background
  • ✅ Well-lit with natural light preferred
  • ❌ No overexposed or washed-out images

Pose & Expression

  • ✅ Neutral facial expression
  • ✅ Both eyes open, looking at camera
  • ❌ No showing teeth
  • ❌ No hair covering eyes

Photo Quality

  • ✅ Color photo, sharp and in-focus
  • ✅ Square JPEG · 600–1200 px · no larger than 240 KB
  • ✅ Taken within the last 6 months
  • ❌ No blurry, grainy, or pixelated images
  • ❌ No photos copied from ID documents

Clothing & Accessories

  • ✅ Daily civilian clothing only
  • ✅ Religious head coverings allowed (full face visible)
  • ❌ No uniforms
  • ❌ No eyeglasses (except documented medical need)
  • ❌ No hats, headphones, or wireless devices

Digital Changes

  • ✅ Original, unedited photo only
  • ❌ No filters, retouching, or digital enhancements
  • ❌ No AI-generated or AI-edited photos
  • ❌ Do not scan or photograph a printed photo
  • ❌ No snapshots, magazine photos, or full-length photographs
🇺🇸

Digital-image guidance: square image, 600–1200 pixels, color/sRGB, JPEG format, maximum 240 KB. For a scanned physical photo: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm) at 300 ppi. Final acceptance is decided by the U.S. Department of State or the relevant U.S. embassy/consulate.
